# My Analytics (User‑Facing)

## What you’ll see
- A clean “My Analytics” screen that focuses on your activity, not scoring.
- Simple numbers that explain what you’ve done during the date range.

## Main sections
1) **Summary cards**
   - Total Transactions
   - Total Quantity Moved
   - Unique Parts Handled
   - Active Days

2) **Trends**
   - A simple chart showing activity by day or week.

3) **Recent Activity**
   - A list of your recent transactions with date, part, quantity, and location.

4) **Glossary**
   - Short explanations for each number so you don’t have to guess.

## Important fairness note
- Using **Advanced Inventory** or **Advanced Remove** counts the same as single entries/removals.
- Those actions are clearly labeled so you can see when they were used.

## Why this is better
- No grades, no scoring.
- Clear, easy‑to‑read totals.
- Quick way to confirm your activity history.

## Delivery Format
- Displayed as an HTML page in the app (same pattern as the Receiving Analytics screen).
- Includes a one‑click Print view designed for letter‑size paper.
